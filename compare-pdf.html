How to use Compare PDF

  1. Add exactly two PDFs to the drop zone below (the first two you add are the ones compared).
  2. Tap Run — the comparison happens automatically.
  3. Review the line-by-line, page-by-page differences in the results.

FAQ

What if I add more than two files?

Only the first two files added are compared; any others are ignored for this tool.

Does it compare formatting or just text?

The comparison works line by line on the extracted text of each page, so it's built to catch wording and content changes rather than subtle visual formatting differences.

Can it compare PDFs with different page counts?

Yes — pages are compared where they exist on both sides, and extra pages on either document are flagged as additions or removals.

Are my files sent anywhere for comparison?

No, the comparison runs locally in your browser tab.
