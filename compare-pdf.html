How to use Compare PDF

  1. Add the original PDF, then the revised PDF (in that order).
  2. Tap Run.
  3. Open the downloaded comparison.html in your browser to review the differences.

FAQ

What if I add more than two files?

Only the first two are compared.

Does this compare images or just text?

Just text. Layout, images and formatting differences aren't detected — only changes to the extracted text of each page.
