About

Why gptpayer exists

A small toolkit, built to stay out of your way.

The idea

Most people don't need a document-management platform — they need to merge two PDFs, shrink a photo, or fix a sideways scan, right now, without creating an account or waiting for an upload bar. gptpayer puts the PDF tools and the image tools people actually reach for in one place, and runs them directly in your browser instead of on a server.

Why "in your browser" matters

When a tool runs client-side, your file never has to leave your device to get the job done. That's not a privacy feature bolted on afterward — it's the reason the tools are built this way. You can read exactly how this works on the Privacy Policy page.

What's finished, and what isn't

Every tool on the homepage is labeled honestly. If it's not marked "Coming soon," it's fully working today — that now includes page organizing, scan-to-PDF, PDF repair, password removal, PDF comparison, PDF-to-Markdown, HTML-to-PDF/image, a small PDF editor, form filling, and on-device background removal. What's still marked "Coming soon" — things like OCR, Word/PowerPoint/Excel conversion, true PDF/A conformance, and connected-AI features like summarizing or translating — needs a full conversion engine or a cloud AI model that a browser tab can't run entirely on its own, so those are listed openly as in-progress rather than quietly faked.

Where this is going

The roadmap is simply: keep the working tools reliable, and turn "Coming soon" into "working" one tool at a time.
