How to use Remove Background

  1. Add a photo with a clear subject.
  2. Tap Run — the first run downloads a small AI model, so it can take a little longer.
  3. Download the transparent PNG.

FAQ

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No — the photo is processed on your device by the AI model. Only the model itself (not your photo) is downloaded from a CDN the first time you use this tool.

Why does the first run take longer?

It's downloading the on-device model. After that first download, your browser typically caches it for faster runs.
