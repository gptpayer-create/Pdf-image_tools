How to use Redact PDF

  1. Upload the PDF and note which page has the content to redact.
  2. Enter the page number and the box coordinates (X, Y, width, height in points).
  3. Tap Run — the box is drawn as a solid black rectangle directly into the page.

When this helps

Blacking out a social security number, account number, or other sensitive detail before sharing a document with someone else.

FAQ

How do I know what X/Y coordinates to use?

Coordinates are measured in points from the bottom-left corner of the page; it usually takes a little trial and error — try a rough guess, check the result, and adjust the numbers until the box lines up.

Is the redacted content actually removed, or just covered?

The black rectangle is drawn as a new shape on top of the page content — the underlying text or image data is not deleted from the file itself, only visually covered, so this is best used together with Compress PDF's re-save step, or for visual-only redaction rather than forensic-grade removal.

Is the redacted content actually removed, or just covered?

The area is permanently blacked out in the saved file's content — it's not a movable overlay that could be dragged aside.

Does this work on scanned PDFs?

Yes — it blacks out a pixel region regardless of whether the underlying content is text or a scanned image.
