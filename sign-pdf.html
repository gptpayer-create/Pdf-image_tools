How to use Sign PDF

  3. Tap Run to download the signed PDF.

FAQ

Is this a legally binding e-signature?

No — this stamps typed text onto the page for convenience; it does not meet the cryptographic or identity-verification requirements of legally binding e-signature standards like eIDAS or ESIGN. For contracts that require verified signatures, use a dedicated e-signature service.

Can I draw my signature instead of typing it?

Not currently — only typed text signatures are supported right now.
