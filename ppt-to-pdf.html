How to use PowerPoint to PDF

  1. Add one or more .pptx files.
  2. Tap Run.
  3. Download the converted PDF(s).

When this helps

Sharing a presentation's content with someone who doesn't have PowerPoint installed, when getting the text across matters more than matching the exact slide visuals.

FAQ

Do images and slide design carry over?

Not in this version — only the title and body text of each slide is rebuilt, one slide per PDF page. Full visual fidelity (images, custom layouts, themes) is on the roadmap.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No — the .pptx is unzipped and read entirely in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.

How many slides can I convert at once?

No hard limit, though very large decks take longer since everything renders in your browser tab.

Can I convert several presentations in one go?

Yes — add multiple files and each is converted independently; you'll get a zip back if you add more than one.
