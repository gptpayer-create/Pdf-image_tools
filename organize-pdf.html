How to use Organize PDF

  1. Add a PDF — thumbnails of every page load below.
  2. Use ↑ / ↓ to reorder, ⟳ to rotate a page, or ✕ to remove it.
  3. Tap Run — the reorganized PDF downloads immediately.

FAQ

Does this touch the original file?

No — your original file never leaves your device and isn't modified. A new, reorganized copy is generated and downloaded.

Can I remove every page?

No, at least one page has to remain in the output file.
