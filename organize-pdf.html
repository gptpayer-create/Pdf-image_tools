How to use Organize PDF

  1. Add a single PDF to the drop zone below.
  2. Drag thumbnails to reorder them, rotate individual pages, or remove the ones you don't want.
  3. Tap Run — the reorganized PDF downloads immediately.

FAQ

Can I work on more than one PDF at a time?

Organize works on one file at a time, since the page thumbnails and drag order are specific to that document. Repeat the process for other files.

Does rotating a page here affect the rest of the document?

No — each page can be rotated independently; rotating one thumbnail doesn't change any of the others.

What happens to a page I delete?

It's simply left out of the saved file — nothing is uploaded anywhere, so the removed page never leaves your device either.

Is there a page limit?

No hard limit, but PDFs with hundreds of pages will take longer to render thumbnails for, since everything happens in your browser tab.
