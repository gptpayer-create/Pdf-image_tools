How to use Blur Area

  1. Upload the image with the area you need to blur.
  2. Enter the X, Y, width, and height of the region, and set the blur strength.
  3. Tap Run to download the image with that area blurred.

FAQ

Does this automatically detect faces?

No — there's no automatic face detection; you enter the box coordinates manually, so you're in full control of exactly what gets blurred.

How strong should the blur be to make something unreadable?

For text or plates, a higher blur strength (25+) is usually needed to make details truly unrecoverable; preview the result and increase the strength if any detail is still legible.
