Pulling a data table out of a PDF report or invoice into a spreadsheet you can sort, filter, or calculate on.

FAQ

Will it get every table perfectly right?

Not always. It's a best-effort heuristic based on where text sits on the page, so simple grid tables come out clean while complex or merged-cell tables may need manual tidy-up.

What about scanned PDFs?

Run OCR PDF first — this tool needs selectable text to detect columns from.

Will it recognize complex, merged-cell tables?

Best results come from simple grid tables; merged cells or tables without clear column alignment may not extract cleanly.

What if my PDF has several tables across pages?

Each page becomes its own sheet in the output workbook, grouping text by position into rows and columns.
