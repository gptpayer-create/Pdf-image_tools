How to use Remove Pages

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. List the pages to remove, e.g. 2, 5-7 for page 2 and pages 5 through 7.
  3. Tap Run to get the PDF with those pages taken out.

FAQ

What happens if I try to remove every page?

The tool blocks that — a PDF needs at least one page left, so you'll see an error if the list you enter covers the whole document.

Do the remaining pages get renumbered?

The page order shifts up to fill the gap, but any page numbers you've printed onto the pages themselves (from a stamp or scan) stay as they were — only Add Page Numbers changes visible numbering.
